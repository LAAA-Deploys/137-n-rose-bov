How We Sell Your Building

A proven 4-step process that creates competition and drives the highest price.

01

Price It Right

Our AI queries 16 government databases in parallel: zoning, permits, code violations, rent control status, flood zones, and demographics. All before your first meeting.

  • Pricing backed by codified benchmarks from 490+ closed transactions
  • 60,000+ buildings tracked across LA and Ventura counties
  • Data-driven valuations, not guesswork
02

Maximum Exposure

Every listing gets multi-channel marketing to thousands of qualified buyers, starting the day it goes live.

  • 100+ targeted buyer calls to 1031 exchangers, recent purchasers, and local owners
  • 36,000+ owner and investor contacts built since 2012
  • Premium placement on CoStar, LoopNet, Crexi, MLS, Redfin, and Marcus & Millichap
  • Professional drone + ground photography. Confidential marketing available.
03

Negotiate the Best Deal

We create competitive bidding among buyers. That's how sellers get the highest price.

  • Offer matrix comparing all offers side-by-side
  • Buyer vetting and proof of funds verification
  • Every offer evaluated on price, terms, contingencies, and certainty of close
04

Close with Confidence

Transparent communication and hands-on management from listing to closing.

  • Escrow timeline management and due diligence packaging
  • 1031 exchange coordination — 100+ exchanges facilitated
  • Weekly Marketing Updates with calls, emails, tours, and candid buyer feedback
